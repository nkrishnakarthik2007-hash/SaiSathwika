# Product catalogue workflow

Send product photos in this chat, individually or in batches. For each product we will prepare: product name, brand, category, variant/size, selling price, stock quantity, product image and availability.

Do not publish guessed prices or stock counts. The admin form will allow the Super Admin and permitted admins to add, edit, archive and upload products. Product data and images will be stored in the dedicated Sai Sathwika Supabase project, not in Git commits.
